YOUR DATA, YOUR INVITATION

Privacy notice.

VivahSutra Free Beta · Effective 16 November 2026

VivahSutra is currently a browser-based invitation maker. This notice explains what stays on your device, which external services are used, and what happens when you choose to share an invitation.

1. Drafts and invitation details

Your invitation text and settings are saved in this browser's local storage so you can return to your draft on the same device and browser. The saved draft includes names, family names, event details, venue, RSVP and your personal message.

In this Free Beta, VivahSutra has no account system, application database or cloud-sync service. These draft details are not sent to a VivahSutra server.

2. Photos

If you add bride or groom photos, your browser resizes them and stores the resulting image data locally with your draft. Photos are not uploaded to a VivahSutra server. They are used in the invitation preview and PNG exports.

3. Sharing and downloads

When you tap Share, the browser prepares the front and back PNG files and uses your device's share sheet when supported. The apps or people you choose in that share sheet receive the content you select. You control that action.

Downloaded PNG files are handled by your browser and device. Check where your device saves downloads.

4. External services and app cache

  • Google Fonts may be contacted to load the selected typefaces when you are online. Google may receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser details.
  • The site is hosted as a static website using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process standard technical request data under its own policies.
  • The app stores its static files and design artwork in your browser's service-worker cache to support faster loading and offline access.

5. What is not enabled

The current Free Beta does not have customer accounts, analytics, advertising, AI generation, cloud sync, a payment gateway, payment verification or a VivahSutra server-side database.

6. Removing your local draft

Your draft and locally stored photos remain in this browser until its site data is cleared. You can clear website data for the VivahSutra site in your browser settings. This may also remove cached app files. Export any invitation you want to keep before clearing site data.

7. Questions and updates

This notice may be updated as VivahSutra adds features. For questions or to report a privacy concern, use the project's GitHub Issues page.
